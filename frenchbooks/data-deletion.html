This page explains how to delete data related to Learn French: Bilingual Reader, operated by Learn Languages Studio Limited. See also the Privacy Policy.

Contact: support@learnlanguagesstudio.com

1. On-device App data

Reading progress, reading time and related statistics (including minutes, reading days, lookup counts, and chapter check-ins), bookmarks, favourites, settings, glosses, and local backups are stored on your device. Clear them in App settings where available, and/or uninstall the App (or clear App storage). We do not keep a separate server copy of this reading data under the current design.

2. Purchases (Apple / Google)

Purchase and restore records are managed by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we cannot delete them for you. To view, request a refund, or manage purchases or subscriptions, use the account and purchase settings in the App Store or Google Play.

3. Support emails

To delete emails or related records you sent us, email support@learnlanguagesstudio.com with subject [Data Deletion] (or “delete my data”), describe what to remove, and we will process the request within a reasonable time and confirm by email, unless the law requires retention.

4. Sharing to other apps

If you share images, videos, or text from the App—via the system share sheet or shortcuts such as Instagram Stories or Threads—the app you choose (for example Instagram, Threads, Facebook, WhatsApp, Messages, and others) handles that content. You decide whether and where to post. To delete something you already shared, use that app. If the platform has app-permission settings (for example on Meta products), you can manage them there. We do not store your passwords for those platforms.

5. Push notifications

If you enabled optional store alerts, turn them off in the App settings and/or your device’s system notification settings, or uninstall the App. We do not maintain a separate user account for push delivery; device tokens are handled through Firebase／Google as described in the Privacy Policy.

1. Données sur l’appareil

Pour supprimer des e-mails ou dossiers liés à vos échanges avec nous, écrivez à support@learnlanguagesstudio.com avec l’objet [Data Deletion] (ou « supprimer mes données »), précisez ce qu’il faut retirer ; nous traiterons sous un délai raisonnable et confirmerons par e-mail, sauf obligation légale de conservation.

Si vous partagez des images, vidéos ou textes depuis l’app—via la feuille de partage système ou des raccourcis (p. ex. Stories Instagram ou Threads)—l’app que vous choisissez (Instagram, Threads, Facebook, WhatsApp, Messages, etc.) gère ce contenu. Vous décidez de publier et où. Pour supprimer une publication déjà en ligne, utilisez cette app. Si la plateforme propose des réglages d’autorisations d’apps (p. ex. produits Meta), vous pouvez les gérer là. Nous ne stockons pas vos mots de passe pour ces plateformes.

5. Notifications push

Si vous avez activé les alertes magasin optionnelles, désactivez-les dans les réglages de l’app et／ou du système, ou désinstallez l’app. Nous ne tenons pas de compte utilisateur distinct pour le push ; les jetons d’appareil sont gérés via Firebase／Google, comme indiqué dans la politique de confidentialité.
